Click on the tree parts to view details.

Leaves

Leaves prepare food through photosynthesis. They absorb sunlight and exchange gases.

Trunk

The trunk supports the tree, holds its branches upright, and transports water and nutrients.

Branches

Branches support the leaves, flowers, and fruits. They help transport water and nutrients.

Roots

Roots anchor the tree in the soil, absorb water and minerals, and may store food.
